# One Member account type; Requester and Responder are per-request roles

There is a single Member account type (plus Admin). Any Member can raise an Emergency Request or accept one; "Requester" and "Responder" describe a Member's role within one request, not an account type. We chose this over separate, verified volunteer accounts to maximise the pool of nearby helpers and keep onboarding to one step, accepting that Responders are unvetted. Admin Moderation and suspension are the abuse backstop.
